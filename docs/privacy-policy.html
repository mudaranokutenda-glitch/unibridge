UniBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

UniBridge helps university students in Algeria find each other to exchange money and to buy and sell items. UniBridge never holds, sends or receives money.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, student inscription number.
  • Profile: wilaya, location you type, profile photo.
  • Verification (optional): photo of your student card. Only you and the UniBridge team can see it.
  • Activity: your requests, chats (text, photos, and a location only when you choose to share it), listings, ratings, reports and blocks.
  • Device: a notification token so we can send you push notifications.

What other users see

Your name, profile photo, wilaya, location, verification badge, rating and member-since date. Your email, inscription number and student card are never shown to other users.

Why we use it

To run the app: matching, chat, marketplace, notifications, trust and safety (verification, reports, disputes). We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, login, files). Push notifications go through Google Firebase. Google sign-in is handled by Google. These services may store data outside Algeria.

Location

The app reads your location only when you press the location button in a chat and confirm. It is read once and sent only to that chat. The app never tracks you.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. When you delete it, your profile, photos, listings, open requests and notifications are deleted. Finished exchanges, chats and ratings stay for the other person, shown as "UniBridge user", because they may be needed to solve disputes. Reports about an account may be kept for safety.

How to delete your account

  1. Open UniBridge and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

If you have an exchange in progress, finish it or open a dispute first. If you can no longer open the app, email us from your account's address and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, delete your account in the app, and write to us to ask for a copy of your data or to correct it (Algerian law 18-07 on personal data).
