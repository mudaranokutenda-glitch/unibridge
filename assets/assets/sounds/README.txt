UniBridge startup sound (optional)
==================================

Put your sound here with this exact name:

  startup.mp3

Format: MP3 (mono or stereo, 44.1 kHz, about 128 kbps).
Length: 1 to 3 seconds. Size: under 100 KB.
Pick something soft; it plays every time the app is opened from scratch.

It plays once, only on a cold start (the app was closed), only if the file exists,
and never when the phone is on silent or vibrate.
People can turn it off: Profile tab -> "Startup sound" switch (on by default).

After you add or change the file: stop the app and run "flutter run" again.
